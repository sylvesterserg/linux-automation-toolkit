# Sylvect inbound campaign kit

## Campaign idea

**Built it fast. Now make it solid.**

This campaign positions Sylvect as the practical technical partner for small
business owners who built an application with AI or no code tools and now need
it to be secure, stable, connected, and ready for customers.

The campaign does not criticize AI builders. It celebrates the fast launch,
then gives the owner a clear next step. The goal is an inbound **App Readiness
Review**, not a vague sales call.

## Audience and promise

**Primary audience:** small business owners and solo founders using tools such
as Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Base44, or v0.

**Trigger moments:**

* The app is about to accept real customer data or payments.
* Spam, bots, errors, or unexplained warnings have started appearing.
* Manual copying between the app, email, calendars, and a CRM is taking time.
* The owner is unsure who controls the code, data, domain, or hosting.
* A demo works, but the owner is nervous about promoting it.

**Plain language promise:** Sylvect reviews what the owner has built, explains
the risks without jargon, and provides a prioritized plan to make it safer,
more reliable, and easier to run.

## Offer and conversion path

Use one offer everywhere so prospects know what to do:

### App Readiness Review

1. A short discovery form captures the app URL, platform, launch stage, and
   biggest concern.
2. A 20 minute fit call confirms scope and access requirements.
3. Sylvect reviews security basics, reliability, integrations, ownership, and
   operational gaps.
4. The client receives a plain language findings summary with **fix now, fix
   next, and watch** priorities.
5. Sylvect can implement the agreed fixes as a separate engagement.

Do not promise that an app is “hack proof,” “fully secure,” or “compliant”
without a defined standard and scope. Use “security review,” “risk reduction,”
and “production readiness” instead.

**Primary CTA:** `Book your App Readiness Review`

**Suggested destination:** `https://sylvect.biz/app-review`

Replace the destination in every asset if the live booking page uses a
different URL. Add campaign tracking parameters by channel, for example:

```text
https://sylvect.biz/app-review?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=make_it_solid
```

can be updated with a global find and replace.

## Four week launch sequence

| Week | Goal | Content | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Recognition | Ownership and “built fast” posts | Save or share |
| 2 | Education | Five checks infographic and security posts | Comment `REVIEW` |
| 3 | Trust | Mini audit walkthrough and anonymized findings | Visit review page |
| 4 | Conversion | Readiness Review offer, FAQ, and objection posts | Book a review |

Post three times per week. Spend 15 minutes before and after each post replying
to relevant founder conversations. Answer comments with useful information
before mentioning the offer. The inbound system only works when the account is
responsive and visibly helpful.

## Production checklist

- [ ] Confirm current logo, colors, typefaces, URL, email, and social handle.
- [ ] Create the `/app-review` landing page and thank you page.
- [ ] Connect the form to a CRM or n8n workflow and send an immediate receipt.
- [ ] Add UTM tags for LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, and email.
- [ ] Export SVG assets to PNG at the platform's recommended dimensions.
- [ ] Add descriptive alt text from each asset's `<title>` and `<desc>`.
- [ ] Test every link and form submission on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Obtain written permission before publishing client names, screenshots, or
      outcomes.
- [ ] Review weekly: qualified visits, form starts, completed forms, booked
      calls, show rate, proposals, and wins.

## Lead response standard

Respond within one business day. A useful first reply is:

> Thanks for reaching out. What did you build it with, is it live today, and
> what is the one thing making you least confident about promoting it?

That question moves the conversation toward a real problem without pressuring
the prospect.

# Permission based outreach and lead handling

The campaign is designed for inbound demand, but selective outreach can place
useful material in front of people who have already shown a relevant need. Do
not scrape lists or send high volume generic messages. Personalize every note
using a public trigger such as a launch post, a request for technical help, or a
referral.

## Warm direct message

> Hi [Name]. Congratulations on launching [App]. I saw that you built it with
> [Tool]. I help small businesses make AI built apps more secure, reliable, and
> connected after the demo stage. I have a short five point readiness checklist
> that may be useful. Would you like me to send it?

## Follow up after permission

> Here it is: [Checklist URL]. Based on your launch post, I would pay particular
> attention to [one relevant check and why]. If you want a second set of eyes,
> the App Readiness Review turns those checks into a prioritized plan. No need
> to decide now. I hope the checklist helps.

## Referral partner note

Use with designers, marketers, fractional operations leaders, and no code
builders whose clients may need technical hardening.

> Hi [Name]. Your clients often move quickly from an idea to a working site or
> app. Sylvect supports the stage after that build: security basics, production
> reliability, ownership, and workflow automation. I would like to send you our
> five point readiness checklist as a resource for clients who ask, “Is this
> ready for real customers?” May I share it?

## One follow up only

Send this five to seven business days later, then close the sequence.

> Hi [Name]. I wanted to place the readiness checklist back at the top of your
> inbox in case [specific concern] is still on your list. If the timing is not
> right, no reply is needed. I will close the loop here.

## Discovery questions

Ask only what is needed to qualify and scope the review:

1. What does the app help a customer do?
2. Which builder, framework, hosting service, and database does it use?
3. Is it a prototype, private beta, or public production app?
4. Does it store personal, health, financial, payment, or other sensitive data?
5. What has already gone wrong or caused concern?
6. Which business step still requires copying or manual follow up?
7. Who controls the code repository, domain, hosting, and database accounts?
8. Are logs, alerts, backups, and a tested recovery process in place?
9. Is there a launch date or customer commitment driving the timeline?
10. What would make the owner feel confident enough to promote the app?

## Fit and boundaries

**Good fit:** an owner can provide authorized access, wants a prioritized
technical review, and understands that remediation is scoped separately.

**Pause and clarify:** the owner requests penetration testing, regulated
compliance certification, legal advice, or access they are not authorized to
provide. Define the appropriate specialist, written authorization, standard,
and rules of engagement before proceeding.

## Lead workflow

1. Capture source, UTM values, app platform, stage, concern, and consent.
2. Send a confirmation email with the expected response time.
3. Create a CRM record and notify the owner through n8n.
4. Qualify for fit before requesting credentials or private information.
5. Use a secure access method. Never request passwords through email or DM.
6. Record the next action and date after every conversation.
7. Ask won clients for a testimonial only after delivering value, and obtain
   written approval for the exact wording and channels.

## Landing page outline

**Hero:** Built it fast. Now make it solid.

**Subhead:** A practical readiness review for small businesses turning an AI
built app into something real customers can rely on.

**Problem cards:**

* **Security:** Find exposed secrets, weak access controls, and missing bot
  protection.
* **Reliability:** Identify silent errors, missing alerts, and production risks.
* **Operations:** Connect handoffs and reduce repetitive manual work.
* **Ownership:** Understand the code, data, hosting, and exit options you
  control.

**Deliverable:** A plain language summary with fix now, fix next, and watch
priorities.

**CTA:** Book your App Readiness Review.

**FAQ prompts:** What access is required? Do I have to rebuild? Can you fix the
findings? How long does a review take? Is this a penetration test? What kinds of
apps are a fit?
